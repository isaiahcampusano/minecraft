# Feature Upgrades (v1.1)

The v1.1 visual-feedback pass adds:

- A fixed center crosshair.
- A reusable wireframe cube around the block under the crosshair.
- Procedural 16×16 block textures with subtle noise and darker tile edges.
- Exponential distance fog and a horizon-to-zenith sky gradient.
- A compact bitmap HUD showing position, flying state, and targeted block type.
- AABB rejection when a placed block would overlap the player.

All UI graphics and glyphs are generated by the renderer, so the game remains self-contained and requires no additional assets or UI libraries.

## v1.3

The v1.3 lighting pass adds:

- A 12-minute day-night clock with smoothly changing sky and fog colors.
- A rotating procedural sun direction and synchronized terrain/player brightness.
- Per-chunk skylight propagation with full recomputation after nearby block edits.
- Darker holes, enclosed spaces, overhangs, and blocks covered from above.
- A 24-hour time readout in the existing bitmap HUD.

Lighting remains fully procedural and introduces no external assets or dependencies.

## v1.4

The v1.4 block-variety and mining pass adds:

- A central block-properties registry with per-material hardness and diggability.
- Timed hold-to-mine interaction, including unbreakable bedrock.
- A ten-stage procedural crack outline that strengthens as mining progresses.
- OAK_LOG and GRAVEL alongside distinct STONE, COBBLESTONE, SAND, and GLASS materials.
- Expanded procedural atlas and hotbar swatches for all ten block values.

Mining progress resets when the mouse is released or the targeted block changes. The system remains tool-free for now and introduces no external assets or dependencies.
